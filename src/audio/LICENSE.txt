Version 7 offline neural speech (FR / DE / EN)
--------------------------------------------
The data URLs in notes-data.js (FR) and international-data.js (DE) are
neural speech generated using Piper Siwis (FR) and Thorsten (DE). English
numeric turns, start-light numbers, GO, crash reactions and legacy eSpeak
supplements now use Piper LJ Speech. They are not human recordings.
Exact model cards, dataset authors, licenses, source URLs and reproduction
steps are in NEURAL-VOICES.md. French generated audio is redistributed with
SIWIS attribution under CC BY 4.0. German and English generated clips are
offered under CC0. The LJ Speech corpus is public domain according to its
model card. No speech model or synthesis executable is bundled.
The old note-*.ogg files remain MIT eSpeak-generated legacy assets and are
not selected by the default language packs in version 7.

English recorded copilot cues
----------------------------
Ten nonnumeric English cues use recorded words from Trigger Rally's AB
voice pack. The other 26 English cues are explicitly marked neural.
Recorded voice author: Andrei Bondor.
License: GPL version 2 or, at your option, any later version.

 Andrei Bondor

The 15 unmodified source WAVs from earlier editions are preserved in
english-source/: Hairpin, Hard, Square, Medium, Easy, Flat, Left, Right,
Finish, Jump, Dont, Cut, Snow, Sand, and Dirt. The Hard / Square / Medium /
Easy recordings are no longer used to approximate numbered notes.
Source repository:
 https://github.com/REDCATUSER/REDCATUSER/tree/main/src/audio/english-source
Recorded playback derivatives join the matching words, normalize/filter
volume and encode mono 22.05 kHz Vorbis. They remain GPL-2.0-or-later.
The full GPL v2 license is in GPL-2.0.txt and embedded third-party notices.
These independently licensed sound assets are aggregated with the MIT game.
The source asset license does not claim ownership of the original speakers.

The default build contains no authentic French or German human recordings.
The settings UI labels voice origin and accepts authorized local voice packs.
Imported packs keep their author's own declared license. Numeric turns use
1 for the tightest bend through 6 for the fastest bend in all three languages.

Music
-----
The original House arrangement is generated locally by app/music.js under
