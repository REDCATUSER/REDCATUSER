The original WAVs are preserved. Playback copies were converted to mono,
32 kHz Vorbis quality 4 with FFmpeg. Engine playback varies with RPM/load;
procedural intake, exhaust, turbo, wind and tire layers supplement the loop.
This is one recorded engine source with differing synthesized characters,
not separate authentic recordings of every selectable real-world car.

Version 6 French and German neural speech
-----------------------------------------
The data URLs in notes-data.js (FR) and international-data.js (DE) are newly
generated neural speech using Piper Siwis (FR) and Thorsten (DE). They are
not human recordings. Exact model cards, dataset authors, licenses and
reproduction steps are in NEURAL-VOICES.md. French generated audio is
redistributed with SIWIS attribution under CC BY 4.0; German generated audio
is offered under CC0. No speech model or synthesis executable is bundled.
The old note-*.ogg files remain MIT eSpeak-generated legacy assets; they
are not selected for French playback in version 6.

English copilot
----------------
The eight supplementary English cues remain MIT eSpeak-generated speech.
22 English cues use recorded words from Trigger Rally's AB voice pack.
Author: Andrei Bondor.
License: GPL version 2 or, at your option, any later version.
Upstream: Trigger Rally 0.6.6.1, data/sounds/codriver/ab/*.wav.
Source distribution:
 https://deb.debian.org/debian/pool/main/t/trigger-rally/trigger-rally_0.6.6.1.orig.tar.gz
Recording distribution:
 https://deb.debian.org/debian/pool/main/t/trigger-rally/trigger-rally-data_0.6.6.1-4_all.deb
Explicit per-file upstream attribution:
 Andrei Bondor
     sounds/codriver/ab/* (GPL)

The 15 unmodified source WAVs used are in english-source/: Hairpin, Hard,
Square, Medium, Easy, Flat, Left, Right, Finish, Jump, Dont, Cut, Snow, Sand,
and Dirt. Those WAVs are included with the game, and in the source repository:
 https://github.com/REDCATUSER/REDCATUSER/tree/main/src/audio/english-source
Playback derivatives join the recorded words, normalize/filter volume and
encode mono 22.05 kHz Vorbis. They remain GPL-2.0-or-later. The full GPL v2
license is in GPL-2.0.txt, and in the embedded third-party notices.
These independently licensed sound assets are aggregated with the MIT game.
The source asset license does not claim ownership of the original speakers.

Difficulty mapping (1 = tightest, 6 = fastest):
 1 Hairpin, 2 Hard, 3 Square, 4 Medium, 5 Easy, 6 Flat, then Left or Right.
There are no authentic French or German human recordings in the default
build. The settings UI labels their synthetic origin and accepts authorized
local voice packs. Imported packs keep the author's own declared license.

Music
-----
The original House arrangement is generated locally by app/music.js under
this project's MIT license. It contains original programmed percussion,
bass and chord patterns, with no samples from commercially released music.
