French copilot phrases
----------------------
note-*.ogg and the data URLs in notes-data.js contain original short French
rally notes synthesized with eSpeak NG 1.52.0's French voice. They are
computer-generated radio-style speech, not professional human recordings.
The generated sound assets and original phrase text are provided under the
MIT license of this project. No eSpeak executable or voice database is
distributed with the game, and no external speech service is used at runtime.

Generation tool: https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng (GPL-3.0-or-later).
The GPL applies to the synthesis tool, not to independently authored phrase
text or ordinary audio output generated from it.

Reproduction:
  espeak-ng -v fr-fr -s 167 -p 38 -a 155 -w phrase.wav "Gauche deux."
  ffmpeg -i phrase.wav -af "highpass=f=210,lowpass=f=4200,acompressor=threshold=0.12:ratio=3:attack=5:release=100,loudnorm=I=-17:TP=-2:LRA=6" -ac 1 -ar 22050 -c:a libvorbis -q:a 2 phrase.ogg

All audio data URLs are embedded locally so the Windows build and standalone
HTML can speak French without Internet access or an installed French voice.


Version 4 international copilot
-------------------------------
international-data.js contains 30 German and 30 English cues. German and
supplemental English cues are generated from the original phrase text in
voice-texts.json using eSpeak NG 1.52.0. They are SYNTHETIC, not human voices.
Their original phrase text and audio output are provided under the project's
MIT license. No speech synthesis executable/database is distributed.

The 15 unmodified source WAVs used are in english-source/: Hairpin, Hard,
Square, Medium, Easy, Flat, Left, Right, Finish, Jump, Dont, Cut, Snow, Sand,
and Dirt. Those WAVs are included with the game, and in the source repository:
 https://github.com/REDCATUSER/REDCATUSER/tree/main/src/audio/english-source
Playback derivatives join the recorded words, normalize/filter volume and
encode mono 22.05 kHz Vorbis. They remain GPL-2.0-or-later. The full GPL v2
license is in GPL-2.0.txt, and in the embedded third-party notices.
These independently licensed sound assets are aggregated with the MIT game.
The source asset license does not claim ownership of the original speakers.

Difficulty mapping (1 = tightest, 6 = fastest):
 1 Hairpin, 2 Hard, 3 Square, 4 Medium, 5 Easy, 6 Flat, then Left or Right.
There are no authentic French or German human recordings in the default
build. The settings UI labels their synthetic origin and accepts authorized
local voice packs. Imported packs keep the author's own declared license.
